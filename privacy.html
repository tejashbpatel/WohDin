Woh Din (वो दिन) – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Woh Din is a nostalgia and reunion app where friends share memories and plan a get-together inside a private group called a Batch. This page explains what the app stores and what it does not.

What we collect

  • Your name – the name you type, shown to others in your Batch.
  • Content you post – stories, jokes, songs and photos you choose to add, with the year you tag, and the "cheers" you give.
  • Batch details – the Batch name, code, reunion dates, venue, your vote and whether you are coming.
  • An iCloud-based user identifier – used only to tell your posts and votes apart from others'. We do not see your Apple ID, email or phone number.
  • Reports – if you report a post, the report is saved so it can be reviewed.

What we do not collect

No location, contacts, advertising identifiers or analytics. We do not track you across other apps or websites, show ads, or sell or share your data. The app accesses only the photos you pick yourself.

Where your data lives

Content is stored in Apple iCloud (CloudKit) under the app's container. Anyone who has your Batch code can join the Batch and see its posts, names and plans, so share the code only with people you trust. Posts are not end-to-end encrypted. A few settings (your name, your blocked list, your chosen vibe) are also kept on your device.

Your choices

  • Delete any of your own posts in the app.
  • Leave a Batch at any time from the Mohalla tab. Leaving permanently removes everything you added to that Batch: your posts and photos, the cheers you gave and received on them, and your name, vote and going status. If you are the last person in the Batch, the Batch itself is deleted too.
  • Report a post or block a person from the post menu.
  • If you cannot use the app, email us and we will delete your data for you.

Retention

Content stays until you delete it, leave the Batch (which removes all your data from it), or ask us to remove it. Deleting the app only removes the on-device settings, so leave the Batch first if you want your data gone from iCloud.

Children

Woh Din is for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes
