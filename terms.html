Woh Din (वो दिन) – Terms & Community Rules

Last updated: October 10, 2026

By using Woh Din you agree to these simple rules. They keep the Batch a friendly place.

Be kind

  • No harassment, hate, threats, bullying or sexual content.
  • Nothing illegal, and nothing that endangers anyone, including children.
  • Post only what you have the right to share. Do not post other people's private photos or details without their permission.
  • No spam, scams or impersonation.

Reporting and removal

Use the post menu to report or block. We review reports and may remove content, and remove people from a Batch, that break these rules. Objectionable content can be reported to tejash.apps@gmail.com, and we aim to act on it within 24 hours.

Your content

You keep ownership of what you post. By posting you allow the app to store and show it to the members of your Batch.

Leaving

You can leave a Batch at any time from the Mohalla tab. Leaving removes all of your posts, photos, cheers, votes and name from that Batch. This cannot be undone.

Batch codes

Anyone with a Batch code can join. You are responsible for who you share it with.

Age
